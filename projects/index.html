Market-ready white-box platform

Rack-Scale Embedded Power and Environmental Control Platform

Designed and built a distributed embedded-control platform integrating Advanced Energy hot-swappable rack power, PMBus telemetry and control, microcontroller-supervised high-current DC distribution, electrothermal fault management, and BLE mesh environmental sensing. The architecture correlated equipment health with downstream environmental behavior rather than treating power delivery, thermal management, and sensing as independent subsystems.
